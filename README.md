# Airport Papers

A reference library of research reports, guidebooks and industry papers on airport planning, design and operations, mostly from the ACRP (Airport Cooperative Research Program), plus ORAT material from consultancies.

60 papers, organised by topic:

| Folder | Topic | Papers |
|---|---|---|
| [01 Terminal Planning & Design](<01 Terminal Planning & Design>) | Terminal sizing, level of service, processing rates, concessions, wayfinding | 12 |
| [02 Airside, Apron & Landside](<02 Airside, Apron & Landside>) | Aprons, air cargo, curbside and roadways, ramps, unmanned aircraft | 7 |
| [03 VDGS](<03 VDGS>) | Visual docking guidance systems | 3 |
| [04 Common Use, Self-Service & Passenger Experience](<04 Common Use, Self-Service & Passenger Experience>) | Common use, self-service, biometrics, touchless journey | 10 |
| [05 IT, Systems & Communications](<05 IT, Systems & Communications>) | Airport IT, systems integration, cybersecurity, PA, NextGen | 11 |
| [06 Emergency, Security & Contingency](<06 Emergency, Security & Contingency>) | Incident response, EOCs, contingency planning, diversions | 5 |
| [07 EVs, Maintenance & Workforce](<07 EVs, Maintenance & Workforce>) | EV charging, asset maintenance, workforce | 5 |
| [08 ORAT](<08 ORAT>) | Operational Readiness, Activation and Transfer | 7 |
